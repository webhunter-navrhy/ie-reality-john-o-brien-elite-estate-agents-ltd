About the property

Description property

Elite Estate Agents are proud to present this fantastic three-bedroom B rated property to the residential sales market. This property is very well proportioned throughout, offering bright and spacious rooms with no compromises, a large patio and garden area to the rear and lots of space to the front with off street parking for 2-3 cars.

The property is very well located on the Portarlington Road within a short distance of Mountmellick town and its many amenities.

The Rock, Emo and Portarlington are all within a few minutes drive of 20 Oak Manor. Junction 15 of the M7 motorway is within 10 minutes drive of this property.

Viewing is very highly recommended.
